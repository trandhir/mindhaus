7. Privacy

Mindhaus runs primarily in your browser. Everything you write — mood logs, journal entries, reflections, thought records, treasured memories — is stored in your browser's local storage on the device you are using.

There are exactly two things that leave your device, and you can read each of them in plain language below. No other analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, or third-party tracking services are loaded.

1. Anonymous visit counter. When you load Mindhaus, your browser sends a single anonymous request to a self-hosted counter so the maintainer can know how many people use the app. The request includes only a randomly-generated visitor ID stored in your browser's localStorage. No IP address is stored, no fingerprint, no cookies, no personal data. The visitor ID expires server-side after 90 days of inactivity. The counter is operated by T. O. Randhir via Cloudflare Workers; the counter endpoint receives your IP address transiently to route the request but does not store or log it. You can opt out by clearing the mh:visitor-id entry in your browser's localStorage (Settings → Site Data, or use the "Reset all my data" link in the footer). Doing so will cause your next visit to be counted as a new visitor.

2. Optional location lookup. The "Use my location" feature in the Local help section — only when you tap it — sends your device's coordinates to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) to identify your country. Coordinates are not stored; only the country code is remembered on your device.

You can clear all your data at any time via "Reset all my data" in the footer.
